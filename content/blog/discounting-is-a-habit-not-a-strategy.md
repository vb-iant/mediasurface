## Discounts compound quietly

A 10% discount approved once becomes the expected starting point for the next negotiation, then the one after that.

## Leaders set the floor, not the exceptions

The leaders who hold pricing best aren't the ones who say no the most — they're the ones who make the exception process visible enough that it stays rare.
