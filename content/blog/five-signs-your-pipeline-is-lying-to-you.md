## Coverage isn't confidence

A 4x pipeline-to-target ratio feels reassuring until you notice half of it hasn't moved stage in six weeks.

## What to check instead

- Stage velocity, not just stage count
- Deals with no next step scheduled
- Deals sitting past their original close date

None of these require new tooling. They require someone to actually look.
