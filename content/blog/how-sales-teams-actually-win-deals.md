## Why process beats talent

Teams that win consistently tend to share a handful of unglamorous habits: a clear definition of what a qualified deal looks like, a shared language for where a deal actually stands, and a habit of reviewing lost deals as carefully as won ones.

## The takeaway

None of this is exciting. That's rather the point.
