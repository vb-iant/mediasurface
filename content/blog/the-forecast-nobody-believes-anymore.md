## A number nobody trusts is worse than no number

Once a forecast has missed enough times, the room stops planning around it — which quietly does more damage than the miss itself did.

## Rebuilding trust starts smaller

A forecast that's right about a narrower window, consistently, earns back more credibility than a wide one that's right by accident.
