## Gut feel doesn't compare across reps

One rep's "confident" is another's "closing next week" — a forecast built on vibes can't be rolled up into a number leadership can plan around.

## Replace the adjective with a stage-exit criterion

A deal is "commit" because it's cleared a defined, checkable milestone — not because someone feels good about the relationship.
