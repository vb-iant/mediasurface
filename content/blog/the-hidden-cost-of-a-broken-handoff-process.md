## Where deals quietly change shape

Between signature and onboarding, terms get reinterpreted by whoever's in the room — usually without anyone deciding that on purpose.

## A handoff is a document, not a meeting

A meeting is forgotten by the time it matters. A written handoff that both sides sign off on survives the gap between sales and delivery.
