## Spreadsheets don't hear objections

Finance can model margin perfectly and still miss the moment a discount request actually shows up in a real conversation.

## Bring the number to the table earlier

Reps who see draft pricing before it's final catch the objections a model can't — and usually improve the number, not just the pitch for it.
