## The account nobody owns yet

Between "deal closed" and "customer onboarded" there's a window where responsibility is genuinely unclear — and unclear ownership is where details get dropped.

## Close the gap with a document, not a hope

A short, specific handoff brief — expectations, timeline, who said what — closes more of that gap than a well-intentioned Slack thread ever does.
