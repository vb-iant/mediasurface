## The tool should disappear

A CRM that takes longer to update than the call itself took is a tool fighting its own purpose. Reps route around friction, not around discipline.

## What actually gets logged

Activity that maps directly to a rep's next action — not activity captured for a manager's dashboard — gets logged consistently. Everything else is optional and gets treated that way.
